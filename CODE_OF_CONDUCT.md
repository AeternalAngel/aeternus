# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; focus on what is best for the project.
- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private
  information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break
these rules, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break them.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/AeternalAngel). Reports are handled confidentially.
